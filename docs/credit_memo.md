# Credit memo: Pampa Energía S.A.

**To:** Credit committee, corporate banking · **Data:** 2023-2025 financial statements in US dollars (2025 20-F) · **Model:** [excel/analisis_crediticio.xlsx](../excel/analisis_crediticio.xlsx)

## Recommendation

**Approve a facility of up to US$ 110 M, 3 years, in US dollars**, preferably as export pre-financing or an amortizing loan,
with covenants of net debt / EBITDA ≤ 3.5x and EBITDA / interest ≥ 2.0x.

- **Why yes:** net debt / EBITDA of 1.1x, a current ratio of 3.1x and cash and investments of
  US$ 1,091 M. Internal rating **BBB** (score 3.55 out of 5).
- **How much:** in the stress scenario, Pampa could add US$ 395 M of debt without exceeding 3.0x net debt / EBITDA in any year.
  The proposed facility is 28% of that capacity.
- **Why not more:** free cash flow was negative in all three years (US$ -463, -157, -376 M) and 2025 debt service coverage was
  0.89x: Pampa repays maturities with new debt, not with its own cash.

Pampa is the least profitable of the three, but the least leveraged and the most liquid. Pampa's Altman Z'' is in the safe zone; YPF and Vista's, in the grey zone.

## Funding

- **It relies on the capital market:** 96% of its debt is corporate bonds (obligaciones negociables, ON) and only US$ 78 M is loans.
  All of its 2025 debt is in dollars and at a fixed rate.
- **It borrows below the sovereign:** its 3 placements in 2025 yielded between 7.25% and 8.125%, below that day's country risk (Emisiones sheet).
- **What a bank adds:** short-term liquidity and export pre-financing, where bonds do not compete. For long tenors, the company chooses the market.
- **Pricing reference:** its Clase 25 bond was placed at 3 years at 7.25%. A facility of similar tenor has to compete with that rate.

## Stress scenario (2026-2028)

Assumptions: sales -20% in 2026 with no recovery, EBITDA margin 5 pp lower, refinanced debt 4 pp more expensive
and capex equal to depreciation. Worst year of each metric:

| Company | Net debt / EBITDA | EBITDA / interest | Debt service coverage | Internal rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pampa | 2.3x | 3.2x | 0.99x | BBB → BB |
| YPF | 3.1x | 3.3x | 1.25x | BB → BB |
| Vista | 2.9x | 3.7x | 1.24x | BBB → BB |

- **Pampa does not breach the assumed covenants, even under stress**, and in the base case its EBITDA could fall 69% before reaching
  3.5x net debt / EBITDA.
- **The real risk is refinancing, but it is not immediate:** under stress, debt service coverage falls to 0.99x.
  Even so, with its 2025 cash (US$ 1,091 M) Pampa can repay everything maturing until 2033 even if the market closes.
  YPF is in the opposite position: US$ 2,355 M matures in 2026, 2.0x its cash.

## Proposed terms

1. Maintenance covenants: net debt / EBITDA ≤ 3.5x and EBITDA / interest ≥ 2.0x, tested semi-annually.
2. Cross-default with outstanding bonds.
3. If pre-financing, assignment of export receivables.
4. Annual review, or earlier if country risk exceeds its 2023 level.

## Risks and limits of the analysis

- **Sovereign and transfer risk:** the company borrows below country risk, but operates in Argentina. This internal rating applies no sovereign ceiling.
- **Maturity concentration:** Pampa repays little until 2028, then large blocks mature:
  US$ 412 M in 2031, US$ 693 M in 2034, US$ 437 M in 2037 (20-F note 12.5.2). The proposed facility matures before those blocks.
- **Stated assumptions:** the covenants, the 3.0x internal limit and the bank's share (30%) are assumptions, not contract data.
- **Own figures:** EBITDA excludes one-off results and associates, so it differs from the adjusted EBITDA the company reports.
